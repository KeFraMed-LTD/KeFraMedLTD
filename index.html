About Us

KeFraMed LTD is dedicated to improving healthcare delivery through the supply of high-quality medical and laboratory consumables.

Founded with a vision to enhance access to reliable healthcare resources, KeFraMed LTD partners with hospitals, clinics, and laboratories across Kenya to deliver dependable and affordable medical solutions.
